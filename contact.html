What ACP cladding specifications and fire-safety grades does Wallmark install in Chennai? +

Wallmark installs certified high-performance Aluminium Composite Panel (ACP) systems engineered for Chennai's coastal environment:

  • Fire-Rated Class A2 Mineral Core: Non-combustible mineral core compliant with NBC 2016 Part 4 for high-rise commercial buildings above 15 meters.
  • Class B1 Fire-Retardant Grade: Mineral-filled core for low-rise commercial elevations requiring strict fire retardancy.
  • Surface Finish: High-durability PVDF (Kynar 500) and FEVE fluoropolymer finishes offering 15–20 year color retention against coastal UV and salt-air exposure.
  • CNC Decorative Panels: Custom laser-cut perforated panels, geometric jaali patterns, and parametric feature screens.

Includes engineered aluminium sub-framing, stainless steel brackets, and Dow Corning structural weather sealants.

How does Wallmark ensure facades withstand Chennai cyclonic winds? +

Chennai is categorized under Zone 5 for Wind Engineering with a basic wind speed (Vb) of 50 m/s (approx 180–200 km/h) under IS 875 (Part 3). Wallmark performs custom Finite Element Analysis (FEA) for every project, calculating design wind pressure based on building height, terrain category, and coastal proximity.

We specify virgin 6063 T6 structural aluminium mullions with moment of inertia (Ixx) calculated to keep maximum deflection below L/175 or 20mm, preventing glass breakage or joint seal rupture during severe cyclones like Vardah or Mandous.

What is the difference between Unitized and Semi-Unitized systems? +

Semi-Unitized (Stick) System: Vertical mullions and horizontal transoms are anchored directly onto the building structure piece by piece on site. Glass panels are then bonded with structural silicone or mechanical toggles. Ideal for buildings up to 6–10 stories.

Unitized System: Complete 4-sided glazed panels are fabricated, assembled, and sealed in our climate-controlled Anna Nagar factory under cleanroom conditions. Panels are transported to site and craned into position floor by floor. This allows 3x faster erection, zero external scaffolding, and superior airtightness for towers above 10 stories.

How do you prevent water leakage during monsoons? +

We engineer all facades using the Pressure-Equalized Rainscreen Principle. Rather than relying on a single exterior silicone bead, our profiles incorporate a dual-chamber drainage system with continuous EPDM gaskets and baffled weep holes. Any rainwater that penetrates the outer line is channeled safely back out through gravity before reaching the building interior.

Every completed project undergoes on-site dynamic water nozzle hose testing following ASTM standards prior to client handover.

Are Wallmark ACP panels compliant with Tamil Nadu Fire Services regulations? +

Yes, 100%. Wallmark strictly complies with the National Building Code (NBC 2016 Part 4 - Fire and Life Safety) and Tamil Nadu Combined Development and Building Rules. We do NOT use cheap flammable polyethylene (LDPE) core panels. For all commercial buildings, we specify certified Class A2 (non-combustible mineral core) or Class B1 (fire-retardant) panels with third-party lab flammability certificates.

What perimeter fire barriers are installed at floor slab edges? +

To prevent vertical smoke and flame spread between floors, Wallmark installs 2-hour fire-rated perimeter fire stops. These consist of high-density mineral wool insulation (minimum 100 kg/m³) retained by galvanized steel back-pans and sealed with elastomeric fire-rated intumescent mastic at every floor slab edge.
