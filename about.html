Wallmark Facades and Structures was established by Ragul R to stand among the best facade installation companies in Tamil Nadu, bringing over 10+ years of dedicated experience in the facade industry and strong engineering knowledge. How We Operate

The 4 Pillars of Wallmark Engineering Rigor

Setting the standard for facade and glazing contractors in Chennai, every project executed by Wallmark adheres to strict international testing standards, certified OEM sourcing, and Indian Building Codes.

01

IS 875 Wind-Load Engineering

Chennai's coastal location subjects high-rises to severe cyclonic winds reaching up to 2.5 kPa (over 200 km/h). Wallmark conducts complete static and dynamic wind pressure calculations for every mullion section, ensuring deflections remain well within strict limits (L/175 or L/240).

02

Documented Detailing & Certified Sourcing

We develop buildable shop drawings, structural calculations, and interface junctions as standard. Materials are sourced directly from audited, top-tier manufacturers (Saint-Gobain, Dow, Hindalco) with documented inward inspection and strict QA verification prior to site installation.

03

Strict NBC 2016 Fire Safety

Safety is non-negotiable. Wallmark only specifies certified Class A2 (non-combustible) and Class B1 fire-retardant aluminium composite panels for commercial elevations. We engineer fire stops and mineral wool perimeter barriers at every slab edge to prevent vertical flame spread.

04

Dedicated Installation & Site QA

Trained glazier, fixer, and rigger crews work under our direct engineering supervision following safe access plans and method statements. We execute laser setting-out, torque-checked anchor fixing, and on-site water penetration testing (ASTM hose tests) prior to handover.
